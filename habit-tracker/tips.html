Ideas for your everyday

Start with what feels manageable
01 💧

Keep a reminder nearby

Keep your water bottle somewhere visible. A small reminder can help you remember your routine.

Stay hydrated
02 📖

Begin with one page

Choose a book you enjoy and start with a single page. Make the first step easy enough to repeat.

Keep learning
03 🌿

Make room for a break

Choose a comfortable time to stand up, stretch, or enjoy a short walk at your own pace.

Enjoy movement
04 🎯

Focus on one small step

Avoid changing your entire routine at once. Pick one habit and give yourself time to practise it.

Start small
05 ⏰

Connect it to your routine

Try reading after breakfast or stretching after work. An existing routine can become your reminder.

Find your rhythm
06 ✨

Celebrate showing up

Notice the effort you made today. If you miss a day, begin again with your next small step.

Be kind to yourself
